# ioredis 'connect ECONNREFUSED' after Kubernetes node upgrade

**Tags:** failover, ioredis, network, node, redis

## Question
After our AKS node upgrade, the Node app logs `Error: connect ECONNREFUSED 10.0.3.15:6379`.
Redis is running fine. Why?

## Accepted Answer
ECONNREFUSED means nothing listens at that IP:port. Your Redis pod likely got a new IP. Use the Service
DNS name, never pod IPs. Check `kubectl get endpoints` to see the current backing IPs.
