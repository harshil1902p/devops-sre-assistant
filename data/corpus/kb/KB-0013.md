# nginx 'upstream timed out (110: Connection timed out) while reading response header'

**Tags:** gateway, ingress, latency, nginx, timeout

## Question
My ingress returns 504 for one endpoint. Logs say upstream timed out. Should I increase proxy_read_timeout?

## Accepted Answer
Only as a temporary measure. The upstream is too slow. Profile the endpoint, fix the query, or make it async.

## Comments
> Async + job polling solved it for us.
