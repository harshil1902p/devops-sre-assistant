# Kubernetes pods intermittently fail DNS with EAI_AGAIN

**Tags:** coredns, dns, kubernetes, network

## Question
Pods sometimes fail to resolve external hosts: `getaddrinfo EAI_AGAIN api.stripe.com`.

## Accepted Answer
Typical causes: CoreDNS overload, conntrack races on UDP, ndots:5 amplification. Scale CoreDNS, use NodeLocal
DNSCache, lower ndots.

## Comments
> NodeLocal DNSCache fixed ours.
