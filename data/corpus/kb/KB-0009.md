# Pod stuck in CrashLoopBackOff - how do I see why it crashes?

**Tags:** configmap, deployment, kubernetes, secrets, startup

## Question
My pod shows CrashLoopBackOff and `kubectl logs` is empty. How do I debug it?

## Accepted Answer
CrashLoopBackOff is a symptom, not a cause. Use `kubectl logs <pod> --previous` to read the crashed
container's output, and `kubectl describe pod` for events and exit code. Common causes: missing env vars or
secrets, bad command, failing migrations, OOMKilled (137).
