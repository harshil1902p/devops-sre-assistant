# How do I fix 'deadlock detected' (SQLSTATE 40P01) in Postgres?

**Tags:** database, locking, postgres, transactions

## Question
Our transfer endpoint sometimes fails with `psycopg.errors.DeadlockDetected: deadlock detected`.
Should we increase deadlock_timeout?

## Accepted Answer
No. `deadlock_timeout` only controls how soon Postgres checks for deadlocks. Fix the ordering: lock rows in
a consistent order (`ORDER BY id FOR UPDATE`), keep transactions short, and retry 40P01 errors.

## Comments
> Sorting the ids before locking eliminated them completely for us.
