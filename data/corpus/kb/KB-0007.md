# Postgres pg_wal folder keeps growing until 'No space left on device'

**Tags:** disk, postgres, pvc, storage, wal

## Question
Our pg_wal directory is 80 GB and the volume is full. Checkpoints run fine. Can I just delete old WAL files?

## Accepted Answer
Never delete WAL by hand; you can corrupt the cluster. WAL is retained by replication slots, `wal_keep_size`,
or failing archiving. Check `pg_replication_slots` for inactive slots and drop them, and fix `archive_command` if failing.

## Comments
> It was a Debezium slot from a connector we deleted months ago.
