# Azure OpenAI 'exceeded token rate limit' 429 - how to handle?

**Tags:** azure-openai, llm, quota, rate-limit, tpm

## Question
We hit `429 ... exceeded token rate limit of your current OpenAI S0 pricing tier`. Retry or more quota?

## Accepted Answer
Both: honour `Retry-After` with backoff, reduce tokens per request, isolate workloads into separate
deployments, and request more TPM or provisioned throughput for steady load.

## Comments
> Splitting batch jobs to their own deployment solved it.
