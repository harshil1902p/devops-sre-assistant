# Kubernetes rolling update causes 502 Bad Gateway for a few seconds

**Tags:** deployment, graceful-shutdown, ingress, nginx, readiness

## Question
Every deploy we see a handful of 502s from ingress-nginx with `connect() failed (111: Connection refused)`.

## Accepted Answer
Endpoint removal is asynchronous; add a preStop sleep (5-15 s), handle SIGTERM, and ensure readiness checks
reflect real readiness.

## Comments
> preStop sleep 10 removed all our deploy 502s.
