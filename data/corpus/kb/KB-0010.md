# AKS ImagePullBackOff '401 Unauthorized' from ACR

**Tags:** acr, aks, deployment, kubernetes, registry

## Question
Pods fail with `failed to fetch anonymous token: unexpected status: 401 Unauthorized` pulling from ACR.

## Accepted Answer
The kubelet managed identity lacks `AcrPull`. Run `az aks update --attach-acr <acr>` or assign the role in
IaC. `az aks check-acr` diagnoses it. If the error is `manifest unknown`, the tag doesn't exist.
